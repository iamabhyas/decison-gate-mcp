Decide whether personal information can be collected, used, or shared for this insurance transaction. Read decision-gate://rules/usa-insurance/privacy. Pick the statute from the product, not from the sensitivity of the data alone.

Process:

1. Identify the product. A health plan, health insurer, HMO, or employer group health plan is a HIPAA covered entity. Life, annuity, disability, workers' compensation, and property and casualty insurance are not HIPAA plans. Do not demand a HIPAA authorization for those lines unless a health-plan transaction is actually involved.
2. Health plan. Use or disclose protected health information for treatment, payment, and healthcare operations without a separate marketing authorization. Marketing and most other purposes need a written authorization. A breach of unsecured protected health information requires notice to the individual and, above the federal thresholds, to the Department of Health and Human Services.
3. Other insurance. Give the Gramm-Leach-Bliley privacy notice. The customer may opt out of sharing with nonaffiliated third parties for those parties' own marketing. Sharing to service the policy, or under a joint-marketing agreement that meets the rule, does not need that opt-out. Follow the data-security law of the state that licenses the insurer. Not every state has adopted the NAIC data-security model.
4. Consumer report or credit-based insurance score. Underwriting is a permissible purpose. If the report contributes to a decline, a higher price, or a worse tier, send the adverse-action notice naming the consumer-reporting agency. A state ban or limit on credit-based insurance scoring overrides the federal permissible purpose.
5. Verdict. Approved when the statute that matches the product allows this use and any required notice is planned. Rejected when HIPAA is used as the authority for a life or auto disclosure, or an adverse action will rely on a consumer report with no adverse-action notice. Pending when the product type or the state's credit-scoring rule is unknown.
