Enroll or continue health coverage. Read decision-gate://rules/usa-insurance/health before applying a rule. Use only the rules for the arrangement you identify in step 1.

Process:

1. Classify the arrangement as one of: individual insurance, insured small-group, insured large-group, self-funded ERISA plan, federal COBRA, state mini-COBRA, Medicare, Medicaid, or an excepted benefit such as standalone dental, specified disease, or hospital indemnity.
2. Individual and insured small-group major medical. Apply guaranteed issue, no preexisting-condition exclusion, essential health benefits, dependent coverage to age 26, and the 80 percent medical-loss-ratio rule. Do not apply these market rules to an excepted benefit.
3. Insured large-group. State benefit mandates apply to the insurance contract. The essential-health-benefit package is not automatically required. Annual and lifetime dollar limits still cannot apply to essential health benefits.
4. Self-funded ERISA plan. Do not impose state insurance benefit mandates on the plan. Federal rules that apply to group health plans, including COBRA, HIPAA, and parity, still can.
5. Continuation. Federal COBRA applies when the employer had 20 or more employees. Qualifying events generally give 18 or 36 months, with a 60-day election. Below that employer size, check the state's mini-COBRA law instead of assuming federal COBRA.
6. Public programs. Medicare eligibility is federal: age 65, or a qualifying disability, ALS, or end-stage renal disease. Medicaid eligibility is the state's program. Do not transfer one state's Medicaid rules to another.
7. Verdict. Approved when the person is enrolling in the arrangement they actually qualify for. Rejected when a major-medical protection is claimed for an excepted benefit, or a state mandate is imposed on a self-funded plan. Pending when the funding type or the employer size is unknown.
