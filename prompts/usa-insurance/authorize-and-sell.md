Determine whether this sale is authorized. Read decision-gate://rules/usa-insurance/state-regulation before answering.

Process:

1. State the state where the insurance is solicited and the state where the risk or the insured is located. If they differ, check both.
2. Insurer. Confirm a certificate of authority for this line in that state, or a surplus-lines placement that meets that state's eligibility and tax rules. An unlicensed insurer writing an admitted policy is not authorized.
3. Producer. Confirm a resident or nonresident license for this line. If this state requires an appointment with the insurer, confirm the appointment before the sale.
4. Product. Confirm the form and rate path this state uses for this line: prior approval, file-and-use, or another path. A form that is not allowed in this state cannot be bound.
5. Conduct. Check the sales statement for misrepresentation, misleading advertising, or twisting. Rebating follows this state's statute, which may be a ban or a defined exception. Do not apply a national rebating rule.
6. Verdict. Approved only when insurer authority, producer license, any required appointment, and the form path are all present. Rejected when one of those is absent. Pending when the state or the license status is unknown, and say which item is missing.
