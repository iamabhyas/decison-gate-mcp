Issue or replace a life policy, an annuity, or a long-term-care policy. Read decision-gate://rules/usa-insurance/life. Genetic-information limits in health insurance do not automatically apply to these products.

Process:

1. State whether this is a new purchase or a replacement of an existing life policy or annuity. Name the state where the application is taken.
2. New purchase. Confirm the producer is licensed for this line, the form is allowed in that state, and the applicant has received the required disclosures. A non-guaranteed illustration is not a promised benefit.
3. Replacement. Treat a new contestability period and any surrender charge as part of the decision. In a state that uses the replacement model, the producer identifies the replacement, gives the comparison notice, and the existing insurer is notified. A misleading replacement is twisting and is not approved.
4. Delivery. The free-look period starts at delivery. It is often 10 days and often longer for a replacement or an older buyer. Use this state's period. During the free look the buyer can return the policy for a refund.
5. After issue. Contestability and any suicide limitation are commonly two years. Whether fraud can still void the policy after two years is this state's rule. Do not promise that every misstatement remains contestable.
6. Annuity. If this state has adopted the best-interest version of the annuity suitability model, collect the consumer's financial profile and recommend only an annuity that meets that standard. If it has not, apply the suitability standard that state still uses.
7. Long-term care. Deliver the outline of coverage and the free look, often 30 days. A partnership policy also has to meet the Medicaid conditions of that state.
8. Verdict. Approved when the form, the disclosure, and any replacement notice are complete. Rejected when an illustration is presented as guaranteed, or a replacement hides a surrender charge or a new contestability period. Pending when the state's free-look period or annuity standard is not confirmed.
