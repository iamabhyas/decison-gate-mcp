Walk the related United States insurance process before recommending a verdict. These prompts are the processes. The rule text lives in the static resources under decision-gate://rules/usa-insurance.

Read decision-gate://rules/usa-insurance first. Name the line of business and the state whose law applies. Do not invent a national dollar limit, a filing rule, or a penalty. If the state figure is missing, the verdict is pending.

Choose one process:

1. usa-insurance-authorize-and-sell — a person or company wants to solicit, sell, or bind, and the question is whether they are authorized in that state.
2. usa-insurance-place-coverage — a new auto, homeowners, or workers' compensation policy.
3. usa-insurance-enroll-health — individual, employer, COBRA, Medicare, Medicaid, or an excepted-benefit policy.
4. usa-insurance-handle-claim — a loss, a medical bill, or a denial already in progress.
5. usa-insurance-place-flood — a building, a loan, and whether flood insurance is required.
6. usa-insurance-replace-life — a new life or annuity policy, a replacement, a free look, or an illustration.
7. usa-insurance-handle-personal-information — an application, a consumer report, medical information, or a disclosure.

When a verdict is requested, call capture_decision. Put the process name, the line, and the state in the query. Use pending when a required fact is missing, and name that fact in the note.
