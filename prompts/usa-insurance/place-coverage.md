Place an auto, homeowners, or workers' compensation policy. Read decision-gate://rules/usa-insurance/property-casualty first. If the property is in a flood zone or secures a loan, also run usa-insurance-place-flood.

Process:

1. Name the line, the state, and the applicant. Run usa-insurance-authorize-and-sell before binding.
2. Automobile. Apply that state's financial-responsibility minimums and any mandatory uninsured-motorist or personal-injury-protection coverage. New Hampshire does not require a liability policy before driving and still requires the at-fault driver to show an ability to pay. Do not copy another state's limits.
3. Homeowners. A homeowners policy is required by a mortgage or lease, not by a general statute. Bind only the perils in the form being sold. Flood and earthquake are not included unless the form or an endorsement says so.
4. Workers' compensation. Treat it as compulsory for the employer in the state where the employee works, then check that state's exemptions. Texas generally allows a private employer to opt out, and an opt-out employer loses the exclusive-remedy protection.
5. Underwrite the application against the filed form. Bind only after the premium basis, the insured, and the effective date are known. Deliver the policy and any proof the state or the lender requires.
6. Verdict. Approved when the line-specific requirement and the form are satisfied. Rejected when a compulsory coverage is missing or the form excludes the peril the applicant thinks is covered. Pending when the state minimum, the employer’s subscriber status, or the form edition is unknown.
